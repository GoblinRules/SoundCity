Studio Max5s HP208 interoperability:
https://us.ugreen.com/pages/ugreen-app
Packet layout, CRC, handshake and exact-model capability/command mappings
were identified by static inspection of UGREEN Audio 2.7.0 (945), with live
encrypted reads verified on HP208 firmware 0.1.5. The C# implementation is
original. No vendor app binary, artwork or decompiled source is distributed.
Research tooling: https://github.com/P1sec/hermes-dec (not distributed).
